# Home Appliance Comparison and Buying Guide Platform

## Capstone Project

### 1. Problem Statement

Purchasing home appliances such as refrigerators, washing machines, air conditioners, televisions, and microwaves can be challenging for consumers due to the large number of available products, brands, features, and price ranges.

Consumers often struggle to compare products based on important factors such as:

- Price
- Brand
- Features
- Capacity
- Energy efficiency
- Customer ratings and reviews
- Warranty
- After-sales service

Existing e-commerce platforms provide a large amount of product information, but users may still need to visit multiple websites to compare products and understand which appliance is most suitable for their specific needs and budget.

This creates **information overload, difficulty in comparison, and uncertainty during the purchasing process**.

### 2. Proposed Solution

The **Home Appliance Comparison and Buying Guide Platform** is a web-based platform designed to help consumers research, compare, and select home appliances more effectively.

The system will provide a centralized platform where users can:

- Browse different categories of home appliances.
- Compare multiple products side by side.
- Filter products based on price, brand, features, capacity, and energy rating.
- View product specifications in a simple and understandable format.
- View customer ratings and reviews.
- Compare prices between products.
- Receive recommendations based on user requirements and budget.
- Access buying guides and product information.
- Understand the advantages and disadvantages of different appliances.

### 3. Aim

The main aim of this project is to develop an easy-to-use platform that helps consumers make **informed, efficient, and confident decisions when purchasing home appliances**.

### 4. Objectives

1. To provide a centralized platform for home appliance information.
2. To allow users to compare multiple products based on important parameters.
3. To reduce the time required to research and compare appliances.
4. To provide personalized product recommendations.
5. To simplify complex technical specifications for users.
6. To provide useful buying guides and customer reviews.
7. To help users select products according to their needs and budget.

### 5. Target Users

The platform is primarily intended for:

- Individuals purchasing home appliances.
- Families looking for suitable household products.
- First-time buyers who need guidance.
- Users who want to compare multiple brands and models.
- Consumers looking for appliances within a specific budget.

### 6. Expected Outcome

The expected outcome of this capstone project is a user-friendly home appliance comparison and buying guide platform that brings **product information, comparisons, reviews, recommendations, and buying guidance** into a single system.

The platform will help users reduce confusion, save research time, and make better purchasing decisions based on their individual requirements.
